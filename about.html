Our Story

From a small wood-fired oven to your neighborhood favorite

Born from Fire

Slice of Fire began in 2015 when our founder, Marco Rossi, returned from Naples with a passion for authentic wood-fired pizza. He believed that great pizza needs just three things: the finest ingredients, a blazing oven, and love.

What started as a tiny street-corner shop has grown into the city's most beloved artisan pizza destination. But our philosophy remains unchanged: every pizza is handmade, every ingredient is thoughtfully sourced, and every customer is family.

Taste the Difference

Our Journey

2015 — The First Flame

Marco opens a tiny pizzeria with a single wood-fired oven and a dream.

2017 — Best Pizza Award

Voted "Best Artisan Pizza" by City Food Critics and featured in Taste Magazine.

2019 — Expansion

We opened our second location and launched online ordering and delivery.

2022 — The Fire Spreads

Reached 10,000 pizzas served monthly and launched our mobile app.

2025 — Today

Three locations, 45 menu items, and a community of pizza lovers we're proud to serve.
